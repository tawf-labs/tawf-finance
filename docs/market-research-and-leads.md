> Deep-research pass to turn the GTM strategy and pitch positioning already
> written this session into actual names, contacts, and a sequenced action
> list. Every lead below is sourced from a live search, dated, and marked

## 1. BPRS leads

### 1.1 BPRS Dinar Ashri, Mataram, NTB. Strongest lead found.

The single largest BPRS in Indonesia by several 2026 sources (RRI, DepositoBPR).
Headquartered Jl. Sriwijaya No.394 Blok X-XI Mataram, NTB, operating since
26 July 2006, several branch and cash offices across Lombok and Sumbawa.
Core capital reported around IDR 15 billion, with IDR 14.75 billion paid in.
This directly validates the S2 segment (NTB and Sumatera Barat) already
identified as the best risk-adjusted target cluster in
`gtm-multi-bprs-strategy.md` §3.7, now with a named institution behind the
province-level statistics. No adverse supervisory findings surfaced in this
research. Being the largest BPRS nationally, it likely already has stronger
governance and IT capacity than a typical BPRS, which should shorten the
IT-vendor governance review named in `regulation.md` §1.

**Next step:** direct outreach, not through an intermediary. Confirm
current leadership and a warm-intro path before cold contact.

### 1.2 PT BPR NTB Perseroda, converting to Shariah. Greenfield opportunity.

A provincially-owned conventional BPR (Perseroda, government-owned)
reported to be accelerating its transformation into a BPRS, per Lombok Post
coverage. A bank actively building a fresh Shariah offering, with no
legacy conventional-product entanglement to unwind, is a different kind of
opportunity than an established BPRS: potentially more open to a modern
rail from day one, since it is building its Shariah product stack from
scratch anyway.

**Next step:** confirm the transformation's current stage and timeline
before approaching. A bank mid-conversion may not be able to commit to a
sell-down pilot until its own Shariah licensing is complete.

### 1.3 BPRS HIK Parahyangan, Bandung, Jawa Barat. Do not approach as a bank partner right now.

This is the bank run by HIMBARSI's own national chairman (see §2), which
would make it an unusually strong lead on relationship grounds alone. But
a press release from the bank itself, dated September 2026, is a direct
clarification about liquidity management and "penyehatan" (recovery)
steps currently overseen by OJK, in response to circulating news. This is
the same pattern already flagged in `gtm-multi-bprs-strategy.md` §3.8 for
two other Jawa Barat BPRS that had their licenses revoked in 2026 (Hasanah
Mandiri, Gaido Indonesia). **Do not add this bank to any near-term target
list until its current supervisory status is independently confirmed
clean.** The relationship value with its leadership, separate from the
bank itself, is still real, see §2.

## 2. Association and channel leads

### 2.1 HIMBARSI leadership, confirmed named individuals

HIMBARSI (Perhimpunan Bank Perekonomian Rakyat Syariah Seluruh Indonesia),
the named association channel already identified in
`gtm-multi-bprs-strategy.md` §4.1, has confirmed 2024 to 2029 leadership:

- **Alfi Wijaya**, Ketua Umum (chairman), also Direktur Utama of BPRS HIK
  Parahyangan (see §1.3, approach the person separately from the bank).
- **Cahyo Kartiko**, Ketua Dewan Pertimbangan, formerly Ketua Umum of the
  BPRS compartment inside ASBISINDO before HIMBARSI's own founding.
- **M. Hadi Maulidin**, Sekretaris Jenderal.

HIMBARSI's own reported sector figures (as of a February 2025 announcement,
cross-check against the March 2025 OJK figures already in
`gtm-multi-bprs-strategy.md` §3.1): assets IDR 24.21 trillion, financing
IDR 18.76 trillion, DPK IDR 16.26 trillion, financing growth of 12.29
percent outpacing conventional BPR's 6.08 percent.

**Next step:** approach the chairman relationship carefully given §1.3.
A conversation about the association channel does not require discussing
his own bank's current situation, but avoid any framing that reads as
using a distressed relationship as the entry point. Consider routing the
first HIMBARSI conversation through Cahyo Kartiko or the Sekjen instead,
depending on which relationship is warmer once checked.

### 2.2 Industry events

**IFN (Islamic Finance news) Asia Issuers & Investors Forum**, a recurring
regional Islamic finance conference with a long attendee list of banks,
asset managers, and family offices across Southeast Asia and the Gulf.
A venue for meeting DPS-adjacent scholars, licensed issuers, and investors
in one place, not just a BPRS-specific channel.

**Next step:** confirm the 2026 or 2027 edition's date and location, and
whether exhibiting or attending fits the budget and timeline better than a

## 3. Sekuritas (licensed issuer) leads

`regulation.md` §1's role table names a Sekuritas partner as the licensed
issuer for any tradable instrument. This research did not surface a
specific Sharia-focused securities firm with a confirmed appetite for a
BPRS-financing tokenization partnership. OJK does publish a periodic list
of licensed securities companies (Daftar Perusahaan Efek, most recently
referenced for March 2026), which is the correct primary source for this,
not a general web search.

**Next step:** pull OJK's current Daftar Perusahaan Efek directly and
cross-reference against firms with an existing Sharia unit or Sharia
securities offering (BRIS's own securities arm, or firms already
underwriting sukuk, are the natural starting shortlist). This is a research
task for whoever owns the Sekuritas relationship, not resolved here.

## 4. Arbitrum ecosystem leads

Confirmed live programs as of September 2026, replacing the defunct RWAIG
already flagged in `pitch-outline.md`:

### 4.1 Arbitrum Foundation Grants Allocator. Directly applicable.

Three tracks, phased, applications reviewed against the current open
phase:

| Track | Grant size | Requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Early / Growth | $20,000 to $60,000 | Minimum 2 milestones |
| Advanced Growth | $60,000 to $150,000 | Minimum 3 milestones, already launched on mainnet, demonstrated growth |
| Multichain | Varies | For projects from other chains integrating with Arbitrum |

Tawf is currently Sepolia-testnet-only, so the Early/Growth track is the
realistic near-term fit, with Advanced Growth as the target once mainnet
launches with real BPRS pools.

### 4.2 Arbitrum Audit Program. The single most directly useful lead in this report.

A $10 million ARB pool specifically to subsidize third-party smart-contract
audits for early-stage projects on Arbitrum, run over a 12-month period.
This maps directly onto the single largest unpriced cost identified in
`docs/pricing-and-infra-cost.md` §5, a real audit quote in the $15,000 to
$50,000 range that currently has no funding source. Subsidized audit
funding from this program, rather than from module margin, is exactly the
kind of path `docs/pricing-and-infra-cost.md` §5 already flagged as
needed ("a grant or CSR conversation, not something the subscription price
should carry alone").

**Next step:** this should be the first Arbitrum-side action taken, ahead
of any grant pitch for the product itself. Confirm current eligibility
criteria and application window directly, program details shift over time
and this was not independently re-verified beyond the source that
referenced it.

### 4.3 Confirmed contact

Alex Speirs, PR and Communications, Arbitrum Foundation,
alexander.speirs@arbitrum.foundation. A press and communications contact,
not a grants reviewer, useful for the awareness side of a pitch (the
already-drafted "#1 by RWA deployment count" hook in `pitch-outline.md`),
not a substitute for the actual grant application channels above.

## 6. Step-by-step next actions, sequenced

Ordered by dependency, not by size. Earlier steps unblock or de-risk later
ones.

1. **Confirm the Golden Gate Ventures and Hijra connection (§5).** Cheapest
   possible action, one internal check against the existing Hijra
   relationship, potentially unlocks the warmest investor lead in this
   report.
2. **Verify BPRS HIK Parahyangan's current supervisory status directly
   before any HIMBARSI outreach (§1.3, §2.1).** Do not let a relationship
   lead walk into a bank-health-risk conversation by accident.
3. **Apply to the Arbitrum Audit Program (§4.2).** Directly funds the
   single largest unpriced cost in `docs/pricing-and-infra-cost.md`, and
   does not depend on Hijra's pool cycle completing first, this can run in
   parallel with the existing GTM sequencing.
4. **Direct outreach to BPRS Dinar Ashri (§1.1).** The strongest new BPRS
   lead with no flagged risk, fits the S2 segment already prioritized as a
   second design-partner conversation in `gtm-multi-bprs-strategy.md` §5.
5. **Route a HIMBARSI conversation through Cahyo Kartiko or the Sekjen,
   not the chairman's own bank (§2.1).** Preserves optionality on the
   association channel regardless of how §1.3 resolves.
6. **Pull OJK's current Daftar Perusahaan Efek and shortlist Sharia-capable
   Sekuritas firms (§3).** Needed before any tradable-instrument work in
   `secondary-market.md` phase 2 can start, independent of everything else
   in this list.
7. **Check PT BPR NTB Perseroda's conversion timeline (§1.2).** Lower
   urgency than steps 1 to 4, a longer-horizon opportunity worth tracking,
   not acting on immediately.
8. **Apply to the Arbitrum Foundation Grants Allocator, Early/Growth track
   (§4.1), once mainnet deployment is on a real timeline.** Sequenced
   after, not before, the audit funding in step 3, since a real audit is
   part of what makes a mainnet-launch grant pitch credible.
9. **Evaluate the IFN Asia Issuers and Investors Forum (§2.2) as a single
   networking motion** covering the Sekuritas, investor, and DPS-adjacent
   leads in one trip, once steps 1 to 6 have produced enough momentum to
   make conversations there concrete rather than exploratory.
